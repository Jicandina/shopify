# Auralis single-product store

## Purpose

Turn Auralis from a sparse concept landing page into a conversion-ready Shopify storefront for one portable countertop extractor. The first hero remains the visual anchor. Every following section must answer a purchase question, not merely add atmosphere.

## Scope

The home page will contain a product purchase rail, visual gallery, plain-language product explanation, verified specifications, included-items area, shipping and returns information, FAQ, and a final purchase action. The product template will gain a gallery, variant controls, price, availability, quantity, dynamic checkout button, payment icons, product detail accordions, and related support information.

## Data rules

Price, images, availability, variants, colors, inventory, and checkout behavior come from the Shopify product created or imported through CJ. No payment credentials, supplier credentials, fake reviews, invented stock counts, delivery promises, performance claims, or medical claims will be coded into the theme. Admin-editable theme settings provide safe default copy while the product is not yet imported.

## Visual direction

Keep the warm mineral palette, graphite text, copper accent, and physical 3D product treatment from the approved hero. Replace the vague fashion-style storytelling below it with product-led composition: a large gallery and an anchored purchase panel, then an alternating visual/specification layout. Information is separated through space, borders, and hierarchy rather than decorative card grids. Motion stays limited to product tilt, image reveals, and product-gallery transitions, with a reduced-motion fallback.

## Shopify payments

The theme will render Shopify dynamic checkout buttons and payment icons when payment methods are enabled. Payment processing itself is configured in Shopify Admin, not in Liquid or JavaScript. PayPal, Shop Pay, and card methods appear only after they are activated by the store owner.

## Verification

Validate JSON configuration, Liquid block balance, theme root structure, responsive CSS behavior, and product-form fallbacks when a product or variant is unavailable. Publish only after the GitHub-connected Shopify theme previews without theme validation errors.
